Monthly Budget & Bill Planner

Know where every dollar goes. 8 printable pages.

Print at home or fill on screen. US Letter. Not financial advice.

How to use this planner

  1. Page 2: write your monthly income and fixed bills first.
  2. Page 3: set a spending limit for each category (try 50% needs, 30% wants, 20% savings).
  3. Page 4: log daily spending for the month.
  4. Page 5: track bill due dates and tick when paid.
  5. Page 6: set one savings goal and shade progress.
  6. Page 7: pay down debts, smallest balance first.
  7. Page 8: review at month end and pick one change for next month.
