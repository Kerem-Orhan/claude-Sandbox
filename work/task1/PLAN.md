# Make $10 in under an hour (user performs all outside actions)

Honest, legal, no upfront cost. Total time about 45 min.

## Plan A: sell unused items (most reliable)
1. (10 min) Walk the home; pick 1-3 items you own and no longer use that plausibly sell for $10+ combined (books, electronics, cables, games, tools, clothing, kitchenware). Do not sell anything borrowed, recalled, or illegal.
2. (10 min) Check price: search the same item on eBay "Sold items" or Facebook Marketplace; price at or slightly under the typical sold price. Aim for $10-15 per item so one sale suffices.
3. (10 min) Take 3 clear photos each (daylight, plain background). Fill in listing_template.md truthfully, including flaws.
4. (5 min) Post on Facebook Marketplace (local, no fees, cash) and/or a local buy/sell group. eBay only if shipping is easy.
5. (10 min) Reply to buyers. Meet in a public place, accept cash or a trusted app payment only after funds show. Never share bank or card details; ignore overpayment or "ship first" requests.

## Plan B (fallback): quick paid favor for someone you know
Offer a real service you can do now (e.g. 1 hour of tutoring, tech help, yard work) for $10+ to a friend or neighbor you message yourself. Be clear about what you offer and price.

## Done when
$10 received for items or work you honestly described.
