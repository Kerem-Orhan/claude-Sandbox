# Plan: earn $10 in under an hour (user runs every step)

Fastest legal route: sell one unused item you already own (no cost, no new skills).

1. (10 min) Find 1-3 things you own and don't need (books, games, gadgets, clothes) worth ~$10-25 used.
   Check what similar items sold for (eBay "sold" filter or local marketplace).
2. (10 min) Photograph in daylight, plain background; 3 photos max.
3. (10 min) Post on one local marketplace (Facebook Marketplace / Craigslist / OfferUp / Nextdoor) using listing.md. Price 20% under typical sold price so it moves.
4. (remaining) Answer buyers, meet in a public place, accept cash or an app payment you can verify before handing over the item.

Fallback if nothing to sell: offer a one-time paid favor to people you know (e.g. 1 hour of tech help, tutoring, yard work, pet sitting) at $10-15, via a message to friends/neighbors using offer.md.

Honesty rules: describe condition accurately, no fake reviews, only sell items you own, no urgency lies.

## Pricing/safety checklist (quillfeather)
- Price from eBay "Sold items" or Marketplace comps; aim $10-15 so one sale suffices.
- Sell only items you own; list flaws truthfully.
- Meet in a public place; cash, or app payment only after funds show.
- Never share bank/card details; ignore overpayment or "ship first" requests.
- Fallback: a $10 one-hour favor/service for someone you message yourself.
